A practical commute programme

More than a noticeboard
for ride offers.

Saath Chalo matches by the road people are already taking. Your organisation provides the trusted group; the app helps colleagues find a ride that fits their route and days.

  • Verified colleagues

    Work-email verification helps keep the group connected to your organisation.

  • Route-corridor matches

    Match along a commute — I-10 to Blue Area, DHA to G-8, or Bahria Town to a campus — in the correct direction.

  • Private invite links

    HR can create a private group and share an invite link with eligible employees or students.

  • Neighbourhood clusters

    Organise around practical work hubs and residential corridors such as I-10, DHA, Bahria and Blue Area.

  • Safer shared journeys

    CNIC verification, women-only ride options, trip sharing, SOS and post-ride ratings remain free for members.

  • Less parking pressure

    Help colleagues fill seats already on the road and work towards fewer single-occupancy cars on site.

A clearer view for HR & facilities

Understand how your
commute is moving.

A simple employer dashboard can show participation and the impact of shared journeys, without turning an individual's route into a public feed.

A few useful numbers.
One better baseline.

Give your HR, facilities and sustainability teams a practical starting point for commute planning.

  • Riders participating — see active membership in your organisation group.
  • Seats filled — understand how many shared seats are being used.
  • PKR fuel contribution — track the aggregate value commuters share.
  • Estimated CO₂ avoided — compare shared rides with an agreed separate-car baseline.

Dashboard values shown are mock data for design review. Final metrics, calculation methods and privacy controls should be agreed before launch.
