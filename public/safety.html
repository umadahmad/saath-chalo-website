The safety basics

Know what each feature does.

We explain the tools plainly, so drivers and riders can make informed decisions before agreeing to share a journey.

  1. 01

    Verify with CNIC

    Complete identity verification before using the community. Verification helps people make a more informed choice about who they travel with.

  2. 02

    Choose women-only rides

    Women can filter for women-only rides. Drivers can mark a posted ride as women-only when they are comfortable offering it.

  3. 03

    Share your live trip

    Send a trip link to family or a trusted contact so they can follow your journey while it is underway.

  4. 04

    Use SOS when needed

    Trigger an alert to the emergency contacts you have added. The alert includes your location so they know where to check in.

Privacy in groups

Your route can be visible
without your name being public.

Workplace and university groups are designed to help people find a commute match without exposing personal details to everyone in the group.

Private until both agree

Start with the commute.
Start a chat by choice.

Members can discover matching routes inside their verified group. Names are hidden in group discovery until both people agree to talk. This gives people a chance to assess route fit before opening a conversation.

  • Group access can be limited to verified work or campus email addresses.
  • Private invite-only groups can be created for an employer or community.
  • Share only the information needed to coordinate a ride.
SOS

When you need help

Keep your emergency contacts close.

Add trusted emergency contacts in advance. During a trip, use the SOS feature to send them an alert with your location so they can respond and check on you.

Important: SOS alerts notify the emergency contacts you have configured. Do not rely on the app as a replacement for local emergency services. In an immediate emergency, contact the appropriate emergency service directly.

Everyone plays a part

A safer ride takes shared care.

  • Before you agree

    Review the route, days, available seats and group context. Ask questions before confirming a ride.

  • Meet thoughtfully

    Agree on a practical public pickup point along the route and let someone you trust know your plan.

  • Leave a rating

    Give honest feedback after the trip. Ratings help other members make better-informed choices.

  • Report a concern

    Use the available in-app reporting and support channels if behaviour does not meet community expectations.
