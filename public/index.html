The difference

Not nearby. On your road.

Most carpool apps match on distance from a pin. Saath Chalo matches on the actual road a driver already drives — so a rider going I‑9 to Blue Area shows up for a driver on I‑11 to Blue Area too, because their roads overlap, in the right direction, before the driver ever leaves.

Questions

Before you ask

Is this a taxi service?

No. Drivers are commuters sharing a seat on a trip they're already making, not for-hire drivers. Contributions cover fuel cost, not a fare.

How is a route match different from "nearby drivers"?

The app checks whether a driver's actual road geometry passes within walking distance of your pickup and drop-off, in your direction — not just whether their starting point is close to yours.

What if I only want women drivers or riders?

Filter search to women-only rides, and as a driver, set front and back seats to women-only, any, or kept empty.

Can my office or university get this first?

Yes — that's the launch plan. Email us and we'll set your organisation up as a verified group.
